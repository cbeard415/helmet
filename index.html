Before you writeProcess & practicalities
Studio or Local Series — which one is for me?
Helmet Studio is for fully crafted productions: detailed planning, full crew when needed, a cinematic finish. Local Series is faster and lighter — the same eye, scaled for venues and local businesses with ongoing content needs. Not sure? Describe the project; we'll tell you honestly which one fits.
How long does a project take?
First drafts are delivered within 6–8 business days after the shoot. We take on a limited number of projects to keep quality where it belongs; faster delivery is possible with a rush fee, depending on availability.
What does a project cost?
Pricing depends on scope, production level and deliverables — Studio and Local Series are priced differently by design. A short message about your project is enough for us to scope it and come back with a clear number.
Is post-production included?
Yes — every project includes editing, color grading and final optimization for web and social. If you prefer, raw footage only is also an option.
Do you travel?
Based in Toronto, working across the GTA — and available for travel depending on the project scope.
